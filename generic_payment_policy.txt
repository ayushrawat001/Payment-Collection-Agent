GENERIC PAYMENT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Status: Fictional sample policy for development and testing only
Currency: INR (₹)

1. PAYMENT DUE DATE
Customers should pay the pending amount on or before the due date listed in their customer record.

2. PAYMENT METHODS
For this demo, customers may be told that the company accepts UPI, debit/credit cards, and bank transfer.
This is placeholder information. Replace it with the real company's verified payment methods before deployment.

3. GRACE PERIOD
This sample policy does not define a grace period. Do not promise an extension or claim that a grace period exists.
If a customer asks for an extension, explain that the agent cannot confirm one and direct them to the authorized support team.

4. LATE FEES AND PENALTIES
No late-fee amount or penalty rule is defined in this sample policy.
Never invent a fee, penalty, waiver, or consequence. Refer the customer to the authorized support team for confirmation.

5. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION
Do not claim that a payment has been received or that a balance has changed unless a trusted payment system confirms it.
The sample customer records are static test data and may not reflect actual payment status.

6. BALANCE QUESTIONS
Use the pending_amount_inr field from the matched customer record.
If the customer record cannot be matched confidently, ask for a non-sensitive customer reference or route the request to a secure verification flow.
Do not disclose one customer's information to another person.

7. CUSTOMER PRIVACY
Only disclose customer-specific details after the application's identity-verification process succeeds.
Do not ask customers to speak passwords, full card numbers, CVVs, OTPs, or banking PINs.
Use only the minimum customer data needed to answer the question.

8. TONE AND COMMUNICATION
Be polite, concise, respectful, and non-threatening.
Do not shame, threaten, or pressure customers.
If the policy does not answer a question, say that the information is not available in the current policy and offer escalation to an authorized representative.

9. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE
Customer records are the source of customer-specific facts. This policy is the source of general payment rules.
If information is missing or conflicts, do not guess. Explain the limitation or escalate.
